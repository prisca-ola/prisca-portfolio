Legalpedia, legal intelligence platform. Case study.

Description

Legalpedia is a legal intelligence platform built for the people and organizations responsible for getting legal work right in Nigeria, from court preparation and judgment writing to law firm productivity, corporate legal response and legal education.

I designed the full product: the marketing website, the user-facing app and the super admin console. The goal was to take a dense, document-heavy domain and give it a clear, confident and modern interface that people actually enjoy working in.
