Legalpedia, legal intelligence platform. Case study.

Description

Legalpedia is an AI B2B legal research platform serving law firms, courts and universities across Nigeria. It’s a centralized digital hub where legal professionals can access judgements, laws of the federation, legal dictionaries, forms and precedents, all organized and searchable in one place.

The Challenge

Legalpedia manages a large, fast-growing body of legal content, judgements, laws of the federation, legal dictionaries, forms and precedents, across many organisations and user roles. The existing admin system had become fragmented and hard to govern, making it slow and error-prone to publish, organise and control access to that data at scale.

My goal was to replace that broken system with a fully redesigned superadmin dashboard that made managing complex legal data straightforward and scalable.

I collaborated on the marketing website and designed the entire super admin solely, covering 17 feature areas including user account administration, organisation management, a full content publishing workflow for all legal content types, real-time platform analytics, support ticket management, access control and audit logging. I interpreted the PRD and delivered the complete design within 2 months. Stakeholders and clients were satisfied with the final product.
