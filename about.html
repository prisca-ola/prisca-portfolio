My design philosophy centers around creating aesthetically pleasing and user-friendly interfaces, with a particular emphasis on accessibility and interactivity. Through my work, I aim to provide meaningful value to users by addressing their pain points and delivering exceptional user experiences.

I stumbled into UI/UX design in 2021 out of curiosity which threw me into the deep end, real products. A year later, I landed at a crypto fintech startup where I learned that good design wasn’t just about clean interfaces; it had to serve the business too. But the turning point came in 2022, during another internship, when I was handed a “mixed reality” design thinking project I had no idea how to approach. That challenge changed everything. I stopped asking “does this look good?” and started asking “does this actually work for people?” I wasn’t just a designer anymore, I was solving problems.

If you ask me about design, I’d say it’s fun and interesting. But if you ask me on a deeper level, I’d say it’s about paying close attention to details, thinking critically about the user’s journey, and making sure every decision adds real value, not just to the user, but to the business too.
